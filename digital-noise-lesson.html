Why does it appear?

The Attention Economy

Apps and platforms don't just want your time — they're built to keep it. Every scroll, like and notification is designed to hold your attention for as long as possible, because your attention has commercial value: the longer you stay, the more valuable you become to advertisers.

One of the most effective tools: FOMO — fear of missing out

“I keep checking my phone because I'm afraid I'll miss something important.”

The real product being sold isn't an app. It's your time and attention.
